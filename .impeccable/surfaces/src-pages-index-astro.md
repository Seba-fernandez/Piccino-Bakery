# Landing Piccino bakery

Scope: the single home page (src/pages/index.astro). Visitor mode: Persuade (portfolio branding piece; the action is "Seguí a Piccino" on Instagram, placeholder handle).
Audience: portfolio readers judging art direction and motion; people who should crave a cookie.
Proof: six synthetic (AI) cookie cut-outs; no claims, prices or reviews.
Constraints: no store UI; Spanish rioplatense; anime.js 4.5.0; reduced-motion path.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cookie you can almost eat through the screen. The page is a pop poster in motion where the photos are bigger than the type, and the signature is a cookie bitten by your own scroll. It refuses the category default: a cream bakery page with a serif, a centred headline and a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched fields that own whole sections, taken from the real @piccinobakery palette: brown #6E3019, terracotta #B4552F, sand #F3E3C6, cream #FFF6ED, chocolate #3B1C11, apricot #E8966A (text on dark only), plus a flavour accent per tile. Bricolage Grotesque (variable, optical sizes) for display and body. Angled panels at -8°, a rotating circular seal, real crumbs, and soft photographic drop shadows. No boxes, no borders.

STORY: The visitor meets Piccino through scale and appetite, sees six flavours with distinct personalities, takes a bite of a classic, understands "simple, real, irresistible", and leaves following the brand.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Brown ground. "Piccino" in sand at ~28vw bleeds off the right edge, with "bakery" under it. A bitten red velvet at ~70vw is cropped bottom-left and tilted -14°. Pistacho is cropped top-right and chips bottom-right. A terracotta panel at -8° sits behind them, with the seal on its corner. The line "Dale amor a tus meriendas." and a sand pill "Conocé los sabores" sit bottom-left above the fold. Slim nav: wordmark plus Sabores, La mordida, Instagram.

FORM: Pop food poster translated to scroll; user-pinned direction (palette "Tomate y cobalto" chosen by the user plus their poster reference), so the concept roll was intentionally skipped as the skill allows for pinned briefs. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
