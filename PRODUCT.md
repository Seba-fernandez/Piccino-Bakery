# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Astro (static output). User asked for Astro or Next.js; delegated pick: Astro, because a single animated landing needs no server and ships as plain static files to Vercel.

## Users
People who see the work as a branding/portfolio piece: potential clients and peers of the designer (Sebas Fernandez) evaluating art direction, motion and craft. Secondary: someone who discovers "Piccino bakery" as a cookie brand and should feel like trying one.

## Product Purpose
A branding landing for Piccino bakery, an artisan cookie brand. It exists as a portfolio/prueba piece, not as a live business: success is a visitor remembering the brand and the motion, not a sale.

## Positioning
Cookies "simple, real, irresistible": big, chunky, artisanal cookies made with real ingredients and love. The page proves it through huge, detailed product imagery and tactile motion (cookies settling into place, a cookie being bitten as you scroll).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Not a store: no cart, prices, checkout, stock or ordering flow.
- Flavours (fixed list and order): red velvet, pistacho, marroc, choco duo (white + dark chocolate), frambuesa, clásica de chips.
- Motion with anime.js v4 (4.5.0), precise and fine.
- Language: Spanish (rioplatense, "vos").

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Piccino bakery. Tagline in use: "Galletas de autor" (the brand's own line on Instagram); "artesanales" is used in supporting copy (footer, meta description). Line: "Dale amor a tus meriendas." Seal: "Simple · Real · Irresistible · Ingredientes reales". "Hechas con ingredientes reales. Hechas con amor."
- Palette taken from the real Instagram (@piccinobakery): chocolate brown #6E3019 and #5F342B, cream #FFF6ED / #F8F1E7, plus terracotta and sand derived for contrast. Real logo: serif small-caps "Piccino bakery" with a line-drawn hand holding a cookie (not yet on the site). Real facts: Córdoba Capital, "galletas de autor", orders one day ahead.
- Layout reference from the user: a poster with angled colour panels and giant cookie photos bleeding off the edges.

## Evidence on Hand
- 6 cookie images, AI-generated (synthetic), background removed: public/img/*.webp|png. The user has some real photos to swap in later.
- Real Instagram: @piccinobakery (Córdoba Capital). No customers, reviews or press to quote: do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. The cookie is the hero: imagery always larger than anything else.
2. Motion must feel physical and earned (weight, settle, crumbs), never decorative noise.
3. Warm, friendly voice; short lines.
4. Honesty: synthetic images and placeholders are labelled for the portfolio reader.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text; full experience degrades to static with prefers-reduced-motion.
